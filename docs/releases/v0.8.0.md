# Leftover v0.8.0

Everything designed on the canvas after v0.7, built test-first: tested on the computer and on a real phone, then attacked and fixed before release.

## New
- **One spending budget.** Plan asks "How much do you want to spend this cycle?", suggests last cycle's spending, then shows what's left, an even-pace marker and a plain status ("On pace. About ₹324 a day is free"). Per-category limits still work.
- **Money timeline.** Home → How? → Day by day until payday: what's in your accounts now, goal money set aside, each bill, EMI and card bill on its date with what's left after it, and payday. It ends on the same number as Home.
- **"Can I afford this?" in today's terms.** Fits in today, a stretch (and how much less every day gets), or waits for payday, with each number it changes. Prices can be typed for bigger buys.
- **Payment screenshots save themselves.** Share a successful payment screenshot to Leftover and it's added with Undo. Money received, failed or pending payments, anything unclear, amounts over ₹50,000, a locked app, likely duplicates and more than 10 in a day still ask first. A switch in Settings turns it off.
- **Accounts can be edited.** Rename, set today's balance (entries stay; the starting balance moves), card days, whether it counts, and hide an empty account.
- **Setup bills are editable.** Every bill chip can be renamed, given an amount and its own due day, or removed; add your own.
- **Profile photo.** Choose from the gallery, take one, change or remove it. Stays on the phone, isn't in backups.
- **Icons everywhere:** tabs, Home shortcuts, Plan sections, categories, Profile and Settings. New categories get an icon from their name.
- **Activity:** swipe right to change an entry's category (with Undo); More filters by category, account, cycle and amount.
- **Entry page:** what was read from a screenshot, "You and Zepto" (times this year, total, usual amount) and similar spends.
- **Goals:** a strip for the goal furthest behind its date, with one-tap fixes.
- **Home states:** no balance yet (asks for one, no made-up number), over until payday (by how much, and what would cover it), and payday.
- **Bank-text notifications** say what saving does to today: "Save it and today goes ₹385 → ₹225". The widget shows today's safe amount.
- **Motion:** the headline number counts to its new value and the ring sweeps; nothing moves with Android's "Remove animations" on.
- **See-through tab bar** with icons and a whole, raised + button.

## Fixed
- Home said "₹193 of ₹7" with nothing left; it now says "Nothing left to spend".
- The raised + button was cut flat by the tab bar; the tabs were unevenly spaced; Plan's Wishlist ran off the screen.
- Hide balances didn't cover Quick add's "safe today" line.
- A budget typed past ₹1 crore showed one amount and saved another.

## Security and robustness (found by attacking the app)
- Shared images are size-checked before reading: a tiny file claiming 100,000 × 100,000 pixels, or a 30 MB+ file, is refused instead of crashing the app.
- The profile photo setting only accepts names the app made, so a tampered backup can't point it at other files.
- The same screenshot shared twice quickly is saved once.
- Hiding an account unhooks bills and repeating splits from it, so nothing can add money to an account you can't see.

## Not in this release
The four-question onboarding with a saving target, and the lock-screen redesign. Setup still asks three things; its bill step is the new editable one.

## Tested
402 unit, integration and journey tests pass on the computer, and all 23 journeys pass on a Motorola Edge 40 Neo (Android 15).

## Install
Download `leftover-0.8.0.apk` and open it on your phone. It's signed with the same debug key as earlier versions, so it installs over v0.7.0 and keeps your data. ARM phones only.
