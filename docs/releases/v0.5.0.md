# Leftover v0.5.0

An early, sideload-only release. Free and open source (GPL-3.0). No account, no internet permission, no tracking.

## New
- **Money you owe:** EMI tracker with prepay what-ifs; credit-card statement cycles with interest-free days, fee-waiver progress, credit-limit use and reward-point expiry; fees and charges in Insights.
- **Cash wallet** with ATM withdrawals and a "count your cash" fix-up. **Irregular-income mode.**
- **Tags and events, quick-add templates, festival funds, repeating shared bills** (rent).
- **Weekly check-in and challenges** (no streaks). **Reminders** for bills, payday, budgets and the weekly check-in, each off until you switch it on.
- **Automatic tracking:** UPI AutoPay register, capture rules, optional payment-app notifications from apps you choose, entries from other apps by link, CSV statement import.
- **Safety:** app lock with your phone's own lock, hide in the app switcher, passphrase-encrypted and scheduled backups.
- **Voice** in English and Hindi, including Hinglish.
- **Settings** regrouped, with screens for Money rhythm, Appearance and language, Privacy and permissions, About and Delete.
- Home, Split, Insights, Activity, SMS and onboarding refinements; larger-text reflow on shared components.

## Known gaps
Receipt scanning, splitting one transaction across categories, swipe actions, a payday/weekly/monthly cycle switch, trip dates on groups, and a widget quick-entry sheet are not built. See the roadmap.

## Install
Download `leftover-0.5.0.apk` and open it on your phone (allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked). This build is signed with a debug key, so it isn't a store-grade release: update by installing the next APK over it only if it uses the same key.

**Not tested on a device end to end.** 218 unit tests pass and lint is clean, but the new screens, notification, biometric and scheduled-job flows have not been driven on a phone. Please report anything odd.
