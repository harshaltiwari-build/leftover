# Leftover v0.7.0

The redesign: the v2 look from the design canvas, built around three numbers, with your own accent colour and font.

## New
- **Safe to spend today** on Home: what was left this morning shared over the days to payday, a ring showing how much of it today has used, and what tomorrow looks like ("Spend nothing more and tomorrow is ₹640", or how an over-spend is spread over the days left). Tapping it opens "Can I afford this?".
- **Home, rebuilt:** "Left until payday" with a "less / more spent than last cycle by today" chip and a Came in · Spent · For goals row; one pace warning when a budget is heading over ("At this pace Food ends about ₹2,000 over; ₹61 a day holds it"); category tiles; the next 7 days of bills as cards with their total; Say it · Afford? · Split · Goals shortcuts. The full breakdown is still under How?.
- **Accent colour and font in Settings → Appearance:** Lime, Lavender, Peach or Sky; Manrope, Bricolage (bold titles and numbers with Manrope for reading) or Plus Jakarta Sans. Everything switches live. Category colours and warnings don't change with the accent, so their meaning stays fixed.
- **Profile:** opened from your avatar on Home or from Settings. Entries logged, cycles tracked, money moved to goals, money kept by waiting, your money rhythm, your year so far, and when you last backed up.
- **Insights is a tab** (Home · Activity · + · Plan · Insights). It opens with Week · Month · Year, four tiles (spent vs the period before, daily average, biggest category, regrets) and, for the month, budget vs actual with Over pace / Watch / On pace against how far through the cycle you are.
- **Plan** starts with how much of your income is already spoken for (bills, EMIs, goals) and what that leaves. Split now lives here and on Home.
- **Quick add:** a strip of the last six days instead of date chips, your most recently used categories first, and the effect on today's safe amount before you save.
- **Softer look:** white cards with soft shadows in light mode (hairlines in dark mode, where shadows vanish) and a floating tab bar with a raised + button.

## Fixed
- Swipe-to-delete's "Deleted" message now shows its Undo button.

## Changed
- New fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and Plus Jakarta Sans, both SIL Open Font License, bundled in the app (licences in `fonts/`).
- Home-screen widgets keep lime and the system font: Android widgets can't load app fonts.

## Not in this release
The four-question onboarding, the money timeline, the merged "Can I afford this?" layout, the widget and lock-screen redesign, a single monthly spending budget setting, and the new onboarding's saving target. They're designed on the canvas and come next.

## Tested
Unit tests pass and the debug APK builds. The new screens have not been driven on a phone yet.

## Install
Download `leftover-0.7.0.apk` and open it on your phone. It's signed with the same debug key as earlier versions, so it installs over v0.6.0 and keeps your data (no database change this time). ARM phones only.
