# Leftover v0.6.0

Reports for any dates, an offline "Ask about your money", and the pieces of the design that were still missing.

## New
- **Share a payment screenshot to add an expense:** share a Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or bank-app screenshot to Leftover (or tap "Read a payment screenshot" in Quick add). It's read on the phone with Tesseract, an open-source text recogniser built into the app, and Quick add opens with the amount, who you paid, the date and time and the UPI reference filled in, and the category you used last time at that place. Failed or pending payments and likely duplicates of a bank-text entry are flagged. "Received from" screenshots come in as income. You check everything and tap Save; nothing saves by itself.
- **Reports for any period:** Today, Last 7 / 30 days, This or Last month, Last 3 months, This year, This or Last cycle, or any dates you pick. Totals, money kept, spend per day, and a comparison with the same number of days before.
- **Charts, or tables instead:** money in vs out (by day, week or month depending on the period), a category donut, and a table for every chart.
- **PDF and CSV export** of any report through the share sheet. A4 PDF made on the phone with Android's own PDF writer; notes are left out unless you switch them on.
- **Needed · Worth it · Regret:** mark any spend from its detail screen. Reports add up your regrets, and Activity has a Regrets filter.
- **Ask about your money:** questions like "How much did I spend on food this month?", "What changed vs last cycle?", "Which bills are due this week?", "Am I on track for Goa?". Worked out from fixed question shapes on the phone, never a language model, and every answer shows the entries behind it.
- **Receipt photos:** attach one from the camera or gallery on any expense. Photos stay in the app's private folder (not in backups), and Activity has a "Has receipt" filter.
- **Swipe left to delete** on Activity, with Undo, and sticky day headers. Split bills and settle-ups still delete from their own page.
- **Recap as an image:** "Share my recap" now sends a picture card, with amounts left out when "Hide amounts when sharing" is on.
- **Leftover PIN:** an optional 4–6 digit PIN, for phones without a screen lock or as a second lock. Only a salted PBKDF2 hash is kept; after 5 wrong tries the pad waits 30 seconds, doubling each time.
- **Trips and events:** dates ("18 – 22 Dec · in 54 days"), a Whole group / My share toggle against the budget, and archiving a group.
- **Widget quick entry:** the widget's Expense / Income buttons open a sheet over the home screen instead of the whole app (the app itself when app lock is on). The Left to spend widget shows "Today ₹… spent".
- **Launcher shortcuts:** long-press the icon for Expense, Income, Say it and Reports.
- **Pure black** dark mode for OLED screens (Settings → Appearance).
- **Two more reminders:** "Log today" at 9 pm on days with nothing added, and a weekly backup nudge after 30 days without one.

## Changed
- Database version 9 (adds the spend feeling and receipt columns). The upgrade is automatic.
- Backups now include each spend's Needed / Worth it / Regret mark.
- The APK is bigger because of the on-device text recogniser (about 20 MB for a release build). Only ARM phones are supported now (arm64-v8a and armeabi-v7a); x86 builds were dropped.
- New dependency: Tesseract4Android 4.8.0 (Apache-2.0), from JitPack, plus the Tesseract English model (tessdata_fast, Apache-2.0) in the app's assets.

## Known gaps
Encrypting the database itself (backups are already encrypted), splitting one transaction across categories, a payday / weekly / monthly cycle switch on Budgets, reading paper shop receipts line by line, and a linked goal on trips are not built. Screenshot reading is tuned to the common UPI layouts and English text; unusual layouts may need the amount typed in.

## Tested
250 unit tests pass, lint has no errors, and the debug and release APKs build. Screenshot parsing is tested on text shaped like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm receipts, but the recogniser itself has **not** been run on a phone yet, and neither have the other new screens.

## Install
Download `leftover-0.6.0.apk` and open it on your phone (allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked). It's signed with the same debug key as v0.5.x, so it installs over v0.5.1 and keeps your data; the database upgrades itself on first launch. Make a backup first if you can. ARM phones only (arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a).
