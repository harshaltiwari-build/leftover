# Leftover v0.5.1

A small fix on top of v0.5.0, found by running the app on a phone.

## Fixed
- Quick add: the "Other date" chip no longer breaks in the middle of a word. Date chips wrap onto a second row when they don't fit, and chips stay on one line except at very large text sizes.

## Tested on a phone
Onboarding, Home, Settings, Reminders screen, cash wallet (ATM withdrawal and cash count), Activity, Split, Plan and the weekly check-in were walked through on a real device. Reminders switches, app lock, notification access and background jobs were not exercised on a device.

Same install notes as v0.5.0: debug-signed APK, sideload only.
