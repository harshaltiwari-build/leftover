Privacy

Your money stays on your phone.

Online only to update

Your entries never leave the phone. The one online feature is the update check you start; it sends nothing about you.

No account, no bank login

Add entries yourself, by voice, from a screenshot, or from bank texts if you allow it.

Locked and hidden

App lock or a Leftover PIN, Hide balances everywhere, and backups protected with a passphrase.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Leftover free?

Yes. Free, no ads, and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Does it need the internet or my bank login?

Not to use it. Leftover has no account and keeps everything in a database on your phone. It goes online for one thing only: checking for a new version when you tap Check for updates (or switch on the daily check). That request sends nothing about you or your money.

Android says it's an unsafe app or blocks the install. Is it?

That's Google Play Protect, which warns about any app that doesn't come from the Play Store and asks for sensitive permissions such as SMS. Leftover uploads nothing; its only online feature is the update check you start. To install it, tap More details, then Install anyway. Releases are signed with Leftover's own key; its SHA-256 fingerprint is in the release notes, so you can check the file is genuine.

Android says "App was denied access" for SMS. Why?

Android 13 and newer block SMS for apps installed from a file instead of the Play Store. To allow it once:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Leftover.
  2. Tap ⋮ at the top right → Allow restricted settings, and confirm.
  3. In Leftover, open Settings → Automatic tracking and switch on bank texts.
Will an update keep my data?

Yes. Install the new APK over the old one and everything stays. Uninstalling first deletes the app's data, so make a backup in Settings → Backup before you ever uninstall.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Leftover is Android only for now.
